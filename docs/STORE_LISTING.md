# Light Tool Library listing

**Name:** Bible

**Package:** `com.lightphone.bible`

**Repository:** https://github.com/faithintheLord/lightphone-bible

**Short description:** Read and study the Bible offline, with saved verses, notes, reading plans, and Strong's word study.

**Description:**

A focused Bible reader for Light Phone III. Download the Berean Standard Bible and World English Bible, then read without an internet connection. Browse books and chapters, resume your reading, search downloaded Scripture, compare translations, and adjust the reading size.

Keep bookmarks, highlights, and notes on your phone. Follow Bible in a Year, New Testament in 90 Days, or Psalms in 30 Days reading plans. Explore BSB word mappings and the bundled Strong's Hebrew and Greek dictionaries.

No accounts, advertising, analytics, or social features. Initial Scripture and word-study downloads require an internet connection. Downloads can resume if interrupted.
